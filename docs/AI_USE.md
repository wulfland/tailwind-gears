# AI assistance and provenance

On September 22, 2026, GitHub Copilot assisted with the initial application implementation, tests, documentation, synthetic product fixtures, fictional meeting transcript, and original procedural artwork generator. The author requested a public repository with the case-sensitive search defect retained for teaching.

The initial automated checks cover unit/API behavior, TypeScript compilation, and Chromium desktop/mobile workflows. Automated checks are not an author or publisher review. The author should review technical accuracy, educational value, originality, rights, and accessibility before chapter delivery and include this assistance in the book's disclosure to Packt.

Product PNGs are generated by `scripts/generate-assets.ts` using Canvas primitives. No AI image-model output or third-party product photos were used. Product descriptions, specifications, pricing, and meeting dialogue are invented exercise data. They must not be represented as real customer evidence or safety certifications.

Third-party components are installed from npm and pinned through `package-lock.json`. Vite supplied the initial scaffold. Lucide icons are under the ISC license with applicable Feather MIT notices; Public Sans and Space Grotesk are under the SIL Open Font License. The build includes the complete upstream font/icon notices without modification. Other dependencies retain their notices in their installed packages.

No manuscript prose or first-edition repository files were changed when creating this sample. The image requirements for manuscript delivery remain separate from these application assets.
